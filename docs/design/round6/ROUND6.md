# Round 6: the panel and where it is

Approved by the owner. This round changes how a step shows its panel and its location. It supersedes parts of round 5, listed below. Everything else in round 5 stands.

`boards/` has every round 6 board in dark and light. The `R6*` files are thin wrappers. `Locator.dc.html` is the shared source for the slide-out and sheets (tweaks: layout, state, theme). `Checklist`, `Tablet`, `Mobile` and `Views` are the round 5 shared sources with an `r6` option added (and `preview` on Checklist and Mobile). With `r6` off they render exactly as round 5.

## The rule that governs this round

The boards are the specification. Match every screen, overlay, label, caption, legend, color, spacing and order exactly, in both themes and at every size.

There is one exception: **panel drawings**. Your renderer's panels are higher quality than the boards' and win. The fuel panel drawn on these boards (temperature gauge, valve lights, crossfeed and pumps) is a stand-in for content and arrangement; the real geometry comes from the pack, drawn by your renderer.

The new **label-free panel preview** is generated by your renderer from the same panel data, at the **same quality** as your full panels: same geometry, same control glyphs and state treatment, same care with stroke weights and spacing, simply without text. Do not copy the boards' outline; it shows intent only.

## Side pane (desktop, wide, tablet landscape) — boards R6PanePre, R6Pane for comparison only

Order: step label and value, Check, If not, caution note, Why with How to do it and Terms, location line, panel preview.

- **Location line:** location icon, "Forward overhead (P5) · Fuel panel", and Where is this?. The whole line is one 44 px target that opens the slide-out with Where it is expanded.
- **Panel preview card:** the whole panel, label-free, at the pane's width, keeping its proportions. Controls the step changes are filled blue; conditional controls dashed; lights outline only. Under it: "Fuel panel · blue: set by this step", Show panel, and the P key hint. The whole card is the button.
- Nothing else is drawn in the pane. The rest of the pane height stays empty; don't stretch the preview.
- R6Pane (a plain Show panel button) is the rejected option, kept for comparison. Build R6PanePre.

## The slide-out (desktop, wide, tablet landscape) — boards R6Panel, R6Where, R6Control, R6TabL

- Slides out from the side pane's left edge, over the step list, at full height below the flight strip. The side pane stays visible and uncovered.
- A scrim dims only the step list.
- **Closes on:** clicking the scrim, the X, or Esc. P toggles it. Focus moves into it when it opens and returns to the control that opened it.
- Uses the pop-over tokens (radius, border, shadow). Its width, its tablet width and the scrim colors are new tokens; take the values from `Locator.dc.html` and cite it as the source. These are approved.
- **Header:** "Step 7 · Fuel pump switches", the panel name as the title, the location line under it, the Esc hint and X.
- **Order, always:** The panel, then a control's card when one is tapped, then Where it is.
- **The panel:** the whole panel, labeled, at 11 px labels or more, never cropped or centered. It fits at desktop and tablet landscape widths. Legend: "Blue: set by this step · ◆ set by your center tank answer · lights drawn unlit · tap a control for details". Open in Flight deck.
- **Where it is:** the plain-language line, the side view, the captain-seat view, the area map, and the "This area / Other panels" legend.
- **How it opens depends on the entry point:**
  - Show panel, the preview, or P: Where it is folded to one line.
  - Where is this? or the location line: Where it is expanded.
  - Tapping a control in the panel: that control's card opens under the panel, and Where it is stays folded.
- **Control card:** name and panel, Positions, In this step (with the decision that set it and Change), What it does, Used in links. The tapped control gets a selection ring, which is solid, never dashed. This card replaces the separate control detail pop-over planned in batch 2.

## Tablet portrait and phone sheets — boards R6TabP, R6PhonePre, R6Phone, R6PhoneCtl, R6PhoneL

- **Expanded step:** the inline drawing is replaced by the panel preview card, which opens the sheet.
- **Sheet:** drag handle, the same header, the same section order as the slide-out, and the step's Check pinned at the foot. Closes on the X, swipe down, or tapping above it.
- **Tablet portrait:** the full labeled panel fits; same as the slide-out.
- **Phone upright:** never shows a squeezed or sideways-scrolling labeled panel.
  - The label-free preview, full width.
  - The step's controls as a list, one full-width 44 px row each: name, setting (mono), and the blue or dashed mark. A row opens that control's card in place.
  - A one-line note: "Turn your phone sideways to see the panel with its labels."
  - Where it is folded.
- **Phone sideways:** the sheet shows the full labeled panel at 11 px labels or more.

## Flow — boards R6Flow

Side pane: the location line (with Where is this?), then the panel preview card, which opens the same slide-out. No large drawing.

## Settings — boards R6Settings

"Map in steps" is replaced by **Panel preview in steps** (On / Off). Show panel and Where is this? always work. Migrate the saved setting: Off becomes Off, and every other value becomes On.

## Keyboard

- P: Show panel, and closes it again.
- W: Where is this?.
- Esc: closes the slide-out or sheet.

Add P and W to the keyboard hint wherever the board shows one.

## The first gate: dense panels

Before building anything else in this round:

1. Render the label-free preview for the three densest panels (captain lights, the MCP, and the densest overhead panel), plus the fuel panel.
2. Render each in both themes, at the 1440 side pane, the tablet landscape pane and the phone sheet.
3. Put each beside your full labeled panel.
4. Send them for the owner's review and wait.

If a dense panel turns into a grid of identical boxes where the layout stops helping, say so and propose options. Don't pick one yourself.
