Design rules for every screen

One set of tokens for both themes and every size. No feature is removed at any size, panel layouts never change, and detail is revealed on demand.

Spacing and sizes

4 8 12 16 24 32 40
  • Header 60 · phase row 60 · Flight strip 48
  • Side pane 520 at 1440 · Flight page section list 232
  • Rows: 12 top and bottom · panels: 16 to 24 · page gutters 32 desktop, 20 tablet, 16 phone
  • Every control and link at least 44 px tall and 44 px wide, including row menus, ? buttons and tabs

Type

32 Page title 24 Section title 20 Panel title 16 Body and step names 14 Secondary text and descriptions 12 Captions and field labels 15 MONO · SETTINGS, VALUES, CODES Capitals only for text you would see in the airplane or its documents: placards, light legends, settings, CDU pages, METAR groups. App labels use sentence case: Check, If not, The controls.

Sizes and scaling

under 600Phone layout 600–899Tablet portrait layout 900–1439Tablet landscape layout (also a half-screen window beside the sim) 1440 and upDesktop layout, scaled up
scale = max(1, min(w / 1440, h / 900)) Never below 100%. 2560 × 1440 gets 160%; 5K at 1× gets 320%. Wide screens never add columns. The checklist column's content stops at 1040 px (before scaling), so lines don't get too long on ultrawide screens.

  • Blue only on what the step changes and on the current step. Values and labels stay neutral.
  • Unlit lights are an outline and legend only. Lit lights take their real color.
  • Selected tabs and segments get a 1.5 px blue ring as well as weight, so they never rely on a pale fill.
  • Text at least 4.5:1, drawing outlines and icons at least 3:1, checked in CI for both themes.

Drawings and words

  • Drawings size by width; height follows. Labels render at 11 px or more, light legends at 10 px or more.
  • Panel layouts come from the pack unchanged. Switches drawn in their real ON direction with OFF / ON labels.
  • Check, If not and Why sit above the drawings and are never hidden.
  • If not says the condition and the action. ? explains why.
  • One name per action: Reset flight plan clears the plan, clearance and notes. Clear all progress (Settings) also clears ticks. Both offer Undo.
  • Flight strip labels match open and closed: Runway, SID, Initial altitude, Squawk, Departure frequency.
